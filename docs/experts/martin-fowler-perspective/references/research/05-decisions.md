## 职业关键决策

### 1. 从英国到美国：不是"跳槽"，是保持独立顾问身份下的迁移

- 教育：生于英格兰 Walsall，就读 Queen Mary's Grammar School；1983–86 在 University College London 取得 BSc(Eng) in Electronic Engineering and Computer Science。`[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/aboutMe.html>
- 毕业后留在伦敦，先后在 Coopers & Lybrand 与一家小型技术公司 Ptech 工作，**1991 年成为独立顾问（independent consultant）**。`[一手]` 同上
- **1994 年移居美国马萨诸塞州，继续做独立顾问**，直到加入 Thoughtworks。`[一手]` 同上
- 2005 年入籍美国，同时保留英国国籍。`[一手]` 同上
- 现居马萨诸塞州 Melrose（波士顿近郊），妻子 Cindy 是结构工程师。`[一手]` 同上

**关键点**：他"去美国"和"加入公司"是两件相隔 6 年的事。迁移本身没有改变他的职业身份（仍是独立顾问）。

### 2. 加入 Thoughtworks：1999 起合作，2000 正式加入（他本人两种说法并存）

- aboutMe 的 Thoughtworks 段落："**I started working with Thoughtworks in the spring of 1999** and found a company whose attitude to people and customers fitted remarkably with my own views." `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/aboutMe.html>
- 同页 History 段落："I moved to Massachusetts in 1994 continuing as an independent consultant **until I joined Thoughtworks in 2000**." `[一手]` 同上
- 2017 年售股文中："When I joined Thoughtworks in **2000**, I did so because I saw a company full of people that shared my view on what effective software development should be like... we've succeeded in growing from the **300 person US company I joined in 2000** to the 4500 person global company that we are now." `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/articles/201708-tw-sale.html>

→ 即：**1999 年开始为 TW 工作，2000 年正式加入**。这不是编造，而是他本人两个页面（甚至同一页面两段）的口径差异，作为"关键转折点年份"值得在 Skill 里标注为「1999 起合作 / 2000 加入，他本人表述不一」。（详见「言行一致 vs 不一致案例」）

### 3. 为什么加入 / 为什么此前不加入任何公司

- 此前 1991 年起做独立顾问，"I'd never imagined joining any of them. This was partly because of the fact that **I wanted the independence to do the writing** that's become an important part of my life, but also because I hadn't come across an organization that was the sort of company I'd like to work for." `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/aboutMe.html>
- 加入的原因："**the key reason I joined them is because they really do believe that people are their biggest asset**. I've seen many companies say that as a cliché, but not here. And that is essential because I've learned that the biggest impact on successful software development is motivated, talented people." `[一手]` 同上
- 2017 年补充："Thoughtworks, however, relied on **hiring capable technologists** who value multi-disciplinary collaboration, and giving them the independence to judge the best solution." `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/articles/201708-tw-sale.html>

**关于"为什么不创业"**：未找到他就此的明确表态（**未核实**）。可用的间接证据只有两条：
- 他放弃了另一次"离开"的机会成本判断——2021 年写道，**"The prospect of a future of frequent speaking was the only thing that's seriously made me consider leaving Thoughtworks."**（即唯一认真考虑过离职的原因是"被要求频繁演讲"，不是"想去创业"）`[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/articles/202106-reducing-speaking.html>
- 2017 年售股后他选择留下："I want to be around as long as I think we can succeed, so I can share in the triumph." `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/articles/201708-tw-sale.html>

### 4. "Chief Scientist"：一个他本人当作反讽的头衔

- FAQ 专条 "Why is your job title 'Chief Scientist'"：**"It's a common title in the software world, often given to someone who has a public facing technical role. I enjoy the irony of it - after all I'm chief of nobody and don't do any science."** `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/faq.html>

### 5. "不做管理者"：明确、反复、并且他自己点出其中的张力

Watching how my colleagues run a business that employs thousands of people, it seems inconceivable to me that I have anything valuable to add. - 对自己的定位极其低调（可复用的"人设"素材）："**I don't come up with original ideas, but do a pretty good job of recognizing and packaging the ideas of others**, or as Brian Foote describes me: 'an intellectual jackal with good taste in carrion'." `[一手]` 同上
- 2025 年与他人合写文章中自述职业身份谱系："When I began in software in the 1980s I was dismissed as an 'object guy' by database folks and as a 'data modeler' by object folks. I've since been dismissed as a 'patterns guy', 'agile guy', 'architecture guy', 'java guy', 'ruby guy', and 'anti-architect guy'." `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/articles/expert-generalist.html>

### 6. 退出公开演讲（2021）：一次典型的"为可支配时间做减法"的决策

- 2021-06-29《Retiring from Speaking》："**I loathe giving talks.**"；"In the future, I am going to try to refuse almost every request to do talks." 并说明他会把省下的精力投到**写作与帮同事写作**上。`[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/articles/202106-reducing-speaking.html>
- FAQ 现状（长期条目）："I have mostly retired from public speaking, as it was never something I enjoyed." `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/faq.html>

### 7. 角色转向"帮别人写"（2021 起）

- "The main thing that's been occupying my brain in the last months has been **working with my colleagues who are working on significant writing works**"（举例：Unmesh Joshi 的 distributed systems patterns、Zhamak Dehghani 的 Data Mesh、Cartwright/Horn/Lewis 的 legacy displacement）。"These authors are much closer to the reality of software development these days than I am." `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/articles/202107-what-doing-now.html>
- 与 FAQ 一致："it isn't part of my role at Thoughtworks to do tool evaluations - indeed the reverse is true... **The best way to get me interested in a tool is when a project or two at TW uses it** and I start hearing good things from the trenches. I've seen (and done) too many demos to believe you can ever learn about the real value of a tool without trying it out on a real project." `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/faq.html>

## 写作与版本决策

### 1. 1999 第 1 版 → 2018 第 2 版：为什么拖了约 19 年

时间线（全部 `[一手]`，出处 <https://martinfowler.com/articles/refactoring-2nd-ed.html>）：

- 第 1 版"**published just before the 20th Century ended**"；refactoring.com 另说他写于 **1997–9**。`[一手]` <https://refactoring.com/>
- 第 2 版：**"published late in 2018"**（`[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/books/refactoring.html>）；2018-11-26 在 informit 上架，2018-12-10 他拿到实体书。`[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/articles/refactoring-2nd-ed.html>
- **拖延的两个理由（他自己给的）**：
  1. **"second editions have a horrible habit of not improving on the original"** ——他"reluctant"修订；
  2. 一个逐渐累积的技术理由：写第 1 版时"classes as the dominant structuring mechanism"正在成为主流，而"如今我们看到其他结构扮演更大角色"，**重构的写法需要"less centered around"类**。
- **动手的触发点**：2015 年与 2016 年初，他先写了一系列"探索各种重构情境"的散文来试探是否值得重写；**到 2016 年中期才正式开始**。他明说这也是他 2016 年后在 martinfowler.com 上写少了的原因："my writing energy has been focused on the book since then."
- 工作量自述："Even with just trying to do no more than replicate what's in the first edition, it's still two years of solid work." `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/articles/refactoring-2nd-ed.html>

### 2. 为什么第 2 版改用 JavaScript

他的决策逻辑是**读者优先**，不是技术偏好：

- "When I choose a language for examples in my writing, I think primarily of the reader." `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/articles/refactoring-2nd-ed.html>
- 筛选条件：① 广泛流行（"among the top half a dozen in language popularity surveys"）② C 系语法（"most programmers would recognize the basic code structure"）。
- 两个候选：Java 与 JavaScript。**选 JS 的理由是"它不完全是围绕类组织的"**——有顶层函数、一等函数常用，"makes it much easier to show refactoring out of the context of classes"。
- **他自己承认这是反讽**："**Choosing JavaScript was deeply ironic for me, as many readers may know, I'm not a fan of it.** It has too many awkward edge cases and clunky idioms." 另：ES6 带来了"rather good class model"，但语言早期遗留的"annoying holes"仍在。`[一手]` 同上
- 他也考虑过"多语言示例"以强调语言中立，但否决："that would be more confusing for the reader."

### 3. 为什么是"原则 + 重构目录"这种结构（以及他 2007 年就写下的"duplex book"理论）

- 第 2 版结构（`[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/articles/refactoring-2nd-changes.html>）：开篇示例 → 原则章 → code smells 章 → 测试章 → **目录（他称之为"the heart of the book"）**。这与第 1 版结构一致："the second edition's structure follows that of the first edition... the first edition was so successful, I might as well continue with what worked."
- **新增**："a chapter of refactorings that contains what I judge to be **a good set of refactorings to learn about first**"（目录前置的入门子集）。
- **删除**："I dropped the later chapters, which explored some more tangential issues... **these days I think it's better to publish essays like this on my website**." 同样理由，他砍掉了 4 个 "big refactorings"，改以网页散文承载。
- **理论出处**：早在 2007 年《Duplex Book》他就提出了这套拆法——**一本书 = 可通读的 narrative book + 可查阅的 reference material**，并以 P of EAA（narrative 106 页）与 xUnit Test Patterns（narrative 181 页）为例。他还给出量化规则："**My personal rule of thumb is that 200 pages is the limit for a narrative cover-to-cover technical book.**" `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/bliki/DuplexBook.html>
- 数字口径（多份自述，注意互相不一致）：2nd-ed 文章说 68 个重构"all but 10 are still present, and I've added 17 new ones"；changes 页列出 **15 个完全新增**；2018-08 备忘说他"新版有 63 个重构"，并因自设页数上限把其中 5 个（19 页）挪到网页版。他自认这些数字是感觉："I'm guessing about three-quarters of it changed, but **that's a gut feel rather than based on a realistic measurement**." `[一手]` 同上三页

### 4. 内容取舍的硬约束

- **不扩大范围**："One of the things I want to stress about this edition, is that **it doesn't cover more scope than the existing book**... I followed a rule of not letting the second edition venture into new topic areas." `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/articles/refactoring-2nd-ed.html>
- **不比第 1 版更厚**：自设上限 412 页（第 1 版页数，不含参考与索引）；初排 440 页，需砍 28 页；用"低优先级 + 无交叉引用"筛出 5 个重构（19 页）下放到网页版，再砍掉一个 10 页的 Java 版 Split Phase 第二示例；**成品 410 页**。`[一手]` 同上
- 他在同一篇备忘里承认"the arithmetic didn't work out quite right"（440−28=412，但最终 410）——自曝算术不严。`[一手]` 同上

### 5. "网页版才是正典"（web-first / canonical web edition）

- "**I think of the canonical form of this book as the web site. The paper book is a selection of material from the web site**, arranged in a manner that makes sense for print. It doesn't attempt to include all the refactorings in the canonical book." `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/articles/refactoring-2nd-ed.html>
- refactoring.com 现状："If you have the book you can access the web edition of the book, **which is the canonical edition. It includes several refactorings not in the book**, as well as an expanded example." `[一手]` <https://refactoring.com/>
- 他对 ebook 的立场是"被行业拖着走"："the book industry doesn't think that way, they expect ebooks to have the same content as the physical books, so the ebook versions of refactoring will follow that principle, **at least for now**." `[一手]` 同上

### 6. 关于"不写新书 / 不写第三版"

- FAQ 专条："**Are you writing another book? — No plans at the moment.**" `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/faq.html>（抓取时长期条目）
- 2021-08："I'm **not tackling a big writing topic, such as a book-length material**... I still have some writing of my own simmering, but I'm keeping it small scale, so that it isn't a tragedy when its pushed aside for a few weeks. Part of me is frustrated that I can't tackle a big topic like I used to." `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/articles/202107-what-doing-now.html>
- **"不写第三版《重构》"的明确表态：未核实。** 现有材料只能支撑"暂无新书计划"+"网页版持续演进"（refactoring.com 称网页版含书中没有的重构）。
- 附带一条他"不做承诺"的自律样本（2018）：谈到以后可能给网页版加新重构时写道"Of course, I thought that before with the first edition, but mostly didn't, **so take that thought with an appropriate amount of salt**." `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/articles/refactoring-2nd-ed.html>

### 7. 心理预期管理：他事先就公开预期"多数人会失望"

《Most people will be disappointed by the second edition》（2018-08-30）：
- 用**损失厌恶**解释："loss aversion means that people feel the loss of something twice as much as they appreciate a corresponding gain. So any improvements in the new edition have to be twice as good as any perceived faults in order for me to break even."
- 并给出判断标准："the true judgment on this new edition isn't the immediate reaction... Instead it's **whether it helps people learn about refactoring in five, ten, twenty years time**." `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/articles/refactoring-2nd-ed.html>

## 技术立场的时间线变化

### 1. Microservices：从"值得认真考虑"到"大多数系统应该做单体"（措辞收紧，非自认反转）

| 时间 | 文本 | 关键表述 |
|---|---|---|
| 2014-03-10 → 03-25 | 《Microservices》（与 James Lewis 合写，**以 9 次分期发布**） | "we do think that **not enough people consider a microservice architecture and that many software developments would be better off if they used it**"；同时"we aren't arguing that we are certain that microservices are the future"、"not enough time has passed for us to make a full judgement"；结尾"**So we write this with cautious optimism.**" |
| 2014-03-25 | 同上，已讨论 monolith-first | "One reasonable argument we've heard is that you shouldn't start with a microservices architecture. Instead begin with a monolith..." |
| 2014-10-20 | Sacrificial Architecture | "**a monolith is often a good sacrificial architecture, with microservices introduced later** to gradually pull it apart"；"I've already run into a couple of projects that took the microservice path without really needing to"；"**I'm wary to recommend** that for a sacrificial architecture." |
| 2015-05-13 | Microservice Premium | "**don't even consider microservices unless you have a system that's too complex to manage as a monolith. The majority of software systems should be built as a single monolithic application.**" |
| 2015-06-03 | Monolith First | "**you shouldn't start a new project with microservices**, even if you're sure your application will be big enough"；结尾自设不确定性边界："**I don't feel I have enough anecdotes yet to get a firm handle... So anybody's advice on these topics must be seen as tentative, however confidently they argue.**" |
| 2015-06-09 | **他在自己站上发表 Tilkov 的反对文章**《Don't start with a monolith》 | "I'm firmly convinced that starting with a monolith is usually exactly the wrong thing to do." 文末致谢："I'm thankful for comments Martin Fowler provided for an earlier version of this post" |
| 2015-07-01 | Microservice Trade-Offs | （标题与日期据内容索引核实）"microservices bring costs and benefits. To make a sensible choice you have to understand these and apply them to your specific context." |

出处：<https://martinfowler.com/articles/microservices.html>、<https://martinfowler.com/bliki/SacrificialArchitecture.html>、<https://martinfowler.com/bliki/MicroservicePremium.html>、<https://martinfowler.com/bliki/MonolithFirst.html>、<https://martinfowler.com/articles/dont-start-monolith.html>、<https://martinfowler.com/tags/>（Microservice Trade-Offs 标题与日期）。全部 `[一手]`。

**判断**：这不是"先鼓吹后打脸"。2014 那篇的自我限定（"certain/cautious optimism"）从一开始就在；2015 年是把"it depends"落到**倾向性结论**（默认单体的正面表述）上。**立场方向一致、推荐强度收紧**。`[推断]`

**他的招牌方法论**（可直接做 Skill 语料）：Microservice Premium 一文以 Kent Beck 的话开篇——"**any decent answer to an interesting question begins, 'it depends...'**"，随后立刻补上"but then I must shift the focus to what factors it depends *on*"。`[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/bliki/MicroservicePremium.html>

### 2. YAGNI：不只是口号，还带成本核算

《Yagni》(2015-05-26) `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/bliki/Yagni.html>：

- 提出四类成本：**cost of build / cost of delay / cost of carry / cost of repair**。
- 引用数据支撑"预判多半错"："even with careful up-front analysis, only ⅓ of them improved the metrics they were designed to improve"（Kohavi et al，微软内部数据）→ 所以"**your odds are at least ⅔**"是白做。
- 划出关键边界（常被误引）："**Yagni only applies to capabilities built into the software to support a presumptive feature, it does not apply to effort to make the software easier to modify.**" 即重构、自测代码、持续交付不算违反 YAGNI——它们是 YAGNI 的前提。"Yagni has the curious property that it is both enabled by and enables evolutionary design."
- 反例自省（承认 YAGNI 会失手）："there are times when applying yagni does cause a problem... My sense is that yagni-failures are relatively rare and their costs are easily outweighed by when yagni succeeds"——并指出这是**可得性偏差**（availability bias）造成的记忆失衡。
- 日常粒度："For similar reasons **I don't add fields or methods until I'm actually ready to use them.**"

### 3. "架构师"角色与"architecture"的定义

- 出处：<https://martinfowler.com/architecture/>（Software Architecture Guide，页面署名日期 1 Aug 2019）`[一手]`
- **重要归属澄清**：那个名句不是 Fowler 说的，是他转述 **Ralph Johnson** 的邮件：
  - Ralph Johnson 的结论："**'Architecture is about the important stuff. Whatever that is'**"
  - Ralph 同时反驳了两种流行定义：①"fundamental organization / 高层组件如何连接"→ 无法客观界定何为"fundamental"或"high level"；更好的说法是 **"the shared understanding that the expert developers have of the system design"**；②"项目早期必须做出的设计决策"→ 更像 **"the decisions you wish you could get right early in a project"**。
  - Fowler 评论："On first blush, that sounds trite, but I find it **carries a lot of richness**."
  - 该邮件后来成为他给 IEEE Software 写的专栏《Who Needs an Architect?》的核心。
- Fowler 自己对"architecture"一词的保留态度："I've long been wary of the term 'architecture' as it often suggests **a separation from programming and an unhealthy dose of pomposity**. But I resolve my concern by emphasizing that good architecture is something that **supports its own evolution, and is deeply intertwined with programming**." `[一手]` 同上
- 对架构的因果判断（可复用命题）："**High internal quality leads to faster delivery of new features**, because there is less cruft to get in the way"；"experienced developers reckon that attention to internal quality **pays off in weeks not months**"。`[一手]` 同上
- 组织立场："Like most people with an agile mindset, **I prefer to err on the side of decentralization, so will head closer to the rocks of chaos rather than suffocating control.**" `[一手]` 同上
- 注：原 PDF `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/ieeeSoftware/whoNeedsArchitect.pdf> 本次抓取失败（内容类型不支持），以上内容来自他站内对该专栏的转述与引用。

### 4. Sacrificial Architecture 与 Strangler Fig：**任务书中的年份需要更正**

- **Sacrificial Architecture 是 2014-10-20，不是 2019**。`[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/bliki/SacrificialArchitecture.html>
  - 核心主张："**often the best code you can write now is code you'll discard in a couple of years time**"；"knowing your architecture is sacrificial doesn't mean abandoning the internal quality of the software"。

《Architecture Decision Record》(2026-03-24) `[一手]` <https://martinfowler.com/bliki/ArchitectureDecisionRecord.html>：

### 1. 他站上真正谈 semver 的文字：**作者不是他**

## 一、句式偏好（每条附原文样本）

### 1.1 主句式：长句裹短句。长句负责铺陈，短句负责落槌

他的默认句法是**长起短收**——用带从句和插入语的长句展开论证，然后用一个 3–6 词的短句砸下结论。这个"长—长—短"的呼吸极稳定。

> "If there's one thing you can guarantee in tech, it's that someone, somewhere, will declare that CSS isn't up to the job of "big projects" and what will undoubtedly be recommended by those same people will be either a JavaScript-heavy approach or some sort of all-in utility class approach like Tailwind."
>
> 中文：技术圈里唯一能保证的事，就是总有某个人、在某个地方，宣称 CSS 干不了"大项目"；而同一批人接下来必定推荐的，不是重 JS 的方案，就是 Tailwind 这类全量 utility class 方案。
>
> —— 一个 56 词的长句，从句套从句，把"对手的论证套路"整段预演完。**紧接着的下一段是**："There's mostly nothing wrong with these approaches"（这些方法基本没问题）。长句建靶，短句缓和。

> "The Cascade is itself, _magnificent_"
>
> 中文：层叠本身，是_壮丽_的。
>
> 来源：[CUBE CSS](https://piccalil.li/blog/cube-css/)｜[一手]｜可信度：高
>
> —— 5 个词成句。这种"极短主谓句 + 斜体形容词"是他的落槌句式。

> "**One job: done well.**"
>
> 中文：一件事：做好它。
>
> 来源：[CUBE CSS](https://piccalil.li/blog/cube-css/)｜[一手]｜可信度：高
>
> —— utility 定义段落的收尾。名词短语 + 冒号 + 极短评断，**无动词成句**。

> "Job firmly done."（活干瓷实了。）／"Job done."（活干完了。）
>
> 来源：[personal-site/10](https://piccalil.li/projects/personal-site/10)、[gorko #20](https://github.com/Andy-set-studio/gorko/issues/20)｜[一手]｜可信度：高
>
> —— 他自己的固定收尾口头禅，用被动式副词加固（firmly）。这是纯文体标记，别处见不到。

### 1.2 排比设问，然后一句话打回去

他写教学文时高频使用**连串修辞问句**，紧接一个极简肯定句：

> "Do you want to build resilient front-ends? Do you want your front-end to work for everyone, regardless of browser and device? Do you want your website to work on poor connections? Do you want your website to work globally?
>
> Have you answered yes to either or all of the above? What you need is **progressive enhancement**."
>
> 中文：你想构建有韧性的前端吗？……以上你答了任何一个"是"吗？你需要的就是**渐进增强**。
>
> 来源：[It's about time I tried to explain what progressive enhancement actually is](https://piccalil.li/blog/its-about-time-i-tried-to-explain-what-progressive-enhancement-actually-is)｜[一手]｜可信度：高

> "'Designers won't work like this'"
> （小标题）
> "Have you communicated that with them, or are you making an assumption?"
>
> 中文："设计师不会这么干活的" —— 你跟他们说过吗，还是你在替他们假设？
>
> 来源：同上｜[一手]｜可信度：高
>
> —— **拿对方的质疑原话当小标题，然后一句反问拆掉。**这是他处理异议的标准武器。

### 1.3 类比密度：高，且是生活化、略带荒诞的英式类比

不是技术类比，是**厨房、房子、汽车、马、食物**这类日常物件。

> "using old knowledge and techniques as the base of a modern approach is like feeding horse feed to a car."
>
> 中文：把旧知识和旧技巧当作现代方案的地基，就像给汽车喂马饲料。
>
> 来源：[CUBE CSS](https://piccalil.li/blog/cube-css/)｜[一手]｜可信度：高

> "Inventing a new button with a `<div>` because there's one or two easily fixable quirks with a native `<button>` is like burning your house down because the windows need a clean. It's a very drastic, unnecessary approach when in reality, all you needed was some soapy water and a sponge."
>
> 中文：就因为原生 `<button>` 有一两个好修的小毛病，就用 `<div>` 造一个新按钮，等于因为窗户该擦了就把房子烧了。现实中你需要的只是肥皂水和一块海绵。
>
> 来源：[Context and caveats](https://archive.hankchizljaw.com/wrote/context-and-caveats/)｜[一手]｜可信度：高
>
> —— **这是他的签名式收尾法：先摆论点，最后一记类比收场。**上一条"马饲料喂汽车"同理。

> "Think of the composition as a **skeleton**."（把 composition 想成**骨架**。）
> "then utilities are the shoes that help it to walk comfortably."（那 utilities 就是帮它舒服走路的鞋。）
> "This isn't a heavily documented, complex methodology... Really, it's more of a thinking structure."（这更像一种思考结构。）
>
> 来源：[CUBE CSS](https://piccalil.li/blog/cube-css/)｜[一手]｜可信度：高
>
> —— 抽象概念一律降维到身体/物件：骨架、鞋、思考结构。

### 1.4 列表：用，但只在"可打包的东西"上

他对列表的使用很克制，且有明确分工：
- **用**：原则、步骤、清单、可枚举的并列项
- **不用**：论证过程。他的论证一律是连续散文，列表只出现在"要点收束"处

> "## Where we stand: our principles
> 1. We will never publish AI-generated content, be it words, code, video, audio and images
> 2. We won't boost content that we suspect has been wholly, or partially generated by AI
> 3. Content submitted by guest authors that is wholly or partially generated by AI will be immediately rejected"
>
> 来源：[Our principles on AI](https://piccalil.li/blog/our-principles-on-ai/)｜[一手]｜可信度：高

> "I tend to follow this order: 1. The main block class 2. Any subsequent block classes 3. Utility classes"
>
> 来源：[CUBE CSS](https://piccalil.li/blog/cube-css/)｜[一手]｜可信度：高

> 日常向的清单则是"生活需求清单"口吻：
> "I'm so fed up of my Apple Watch, so has anyone got a recommendation for an alarm clock that: 1. Is _chill_ with the sounds. I don't need to be yelled awake thanks. 2. Allows me to set a different time alarm — or no alarm — for different days 3. Is not smart and never connects to the internet 4. Doesn't _tick_"
>
> 中文：我受够 Apple Watch 了，有没有人推荐一款闹钟，满足：1. 声音_温和_，我不想被吼醒，谢谢。2. 能按天设不同闹钟或不设。3. 不智能、永不联网。4. 不_滴答_响。
>
> 来源：[I want an alarm clock](https://bell.bz/i-want-an-alarm-clock/)｜[一手]｜可信度：高

### 1.5 破折号与标点癖

- **em dash（——）高密度使用**，用于插入补充、转折、自我吐槽。
- **加粗用于钉住术语和强调句**，斜体用于语气（尤其反讽和外来词）。
- **"y'know"、"innit"、"vibe"-级口语标记**低频出现，用于拉近距离。
- **斜杠 `/` 偶尔当并列符用**："cycles/iterations/sprints"。

> "CUBE stands for **Composition Utility Block Exception**, and CSS stands for, y'know, CSS (Cascading Style Sheets)."
>
> 来源：[CUBE CSS](https://piccalil.li/blog/cube-css/)｜[一手]｜可信度：高

> "The tech choices simply are not important until that is sorted."
> / "Working in the actual deliverable's medium — the web — in cycles/iterations/sprints"
>
> 来源：[progressive enhancement 文](https://piccalil.li/blog/its-about-time-i-tried-to-explain-what-progressive-enhancement-actually-is)｜[一手]｜可信度：高

> "Code language js / Copy _to clipboard_"——他站内代码块的固定 UI 文案，"Copy to clipboard" 全程斜体。属站点文案，非他个人语体，仅作标点习惯旁证。

## 三、禁忌词 / 他刻意回避的表达

以下为**从原文反向推定**的回避项——每条都给出他实际用到替代说法的一手样本，而非单纯猜测。

| 他回避的表达 | 证据（他选了什么） |
|---|---|
| **"best practice"（最佳实践）** | 从不用这个词背书。他改用"works for us"、"a good example"、"I recommend"。样本："This is a boilerplate that **works for us**, here at Set Studio" [README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Set-Creative-Studio/cube-boilerplate/main/README.md) |
| **"you must / you should always"（你必须）** | 换成"all I would recommend"、"I would issue a warning though"、"this could help"。样本："**Any method is extremely valid for your context** and if this is how you write your CSS, **keep doing what works for you and your team**." [CUBE CSS](https://piccalil.li/blog/cube-css/) |
| **"just"（"只要…就行"式简化词）** | 他反而**显式嘲笑**这个词把问题说小了："you can **simplify _a lot_** by not chucking designs over the fence"——用加重副词抵消，不靠 just。 |
| **"obviously / clearly"（显然）** | 从不用来压人。他改用"to be honest"、"I think"、"I reckon" 这类**自降确定性**的垫话。 |
| **"ROI / leverage / synergy / scalable solution"（商业黑话）** | 极罕见。仅一次使用 "ROI" 但带贬义上下文："When the real costs hit, the ROI just simply isn't there." [LLM 文](https://bell.bz/the-logical-destination-of-llms/)；"scalable" 出现时他自嘲："sorry I said it again" [CUBE CSS](https://piccalil.li/blog/cube-css/) |
| **被动语态甩锅（"it was decided"）** | 几乎全用第一人称主动："**I** propose"、"**I'd** recommend"、"**My** recommendation was almost never…" |
| **对方法论的神化措辞（"the right way"）** | 明确反对："before you recommend **your way** as absolute" [CUBE CSS](https://piccalil.li/blog/cube-css/) |
| **贬称对手（"legacy devs"、"Tailwind bros"）** | 未取得任何指涉对手群体的贬称样本。批评一律指向**做法**，不指向**人群身份**——见第八节。 |

## 四、节奏感与结构习惯

### 4.1 结论先行，但先给"共同前提"

他的技术文标准骨架是：**挑衅性开场判断 → 让步 → 上下文/数据 → 我的做法 → 免责（不是命令）→ 类比收尾**。

> "If there's one thing you can guarantee in tech, it's that someone, somewhere, will declare that CSS isn't up to the job of 'big projects'…"（开场：点出行业套路）
> "There's mostly nothing wrong with these approaches—I quite like Tailwind for early-stage prototyping—but often the context… is conveniently **left out**"（让步＋反转）
> "For context, it's estimated that WordPress powers around 36% of the internet… Compare that to a paltry **0.3%** of websites that use React"（数据）
> "In this article, I'm going to tell you about how **I like to write CSS**"（限定为"我的做法"，不是"对的"）
> 结尾："**Any method is extremely valid for your context**"（免责）＋"Enough of an introduction, let's dig in."（推进）
>
> 来源：[CUBE CSS](https://piccalil.li/blog/cube-css/)｜[一手]｜可信度：高

### 4.2 转折方式：三种

1. **but 的"但是被漏掉了"式**：不否定对方，指出对方漏了前提。
   > "but often the context of an all-in JavaScript project… is conveniently **left out**, at the point of suggestion."
2. **自嘲式转折**（把话锋转向自己，避免指责读者）：
   > "I mention all of this, not to sound all smart about design systems, but because…"
   > "sorry I said it again"（又一次用了"scalable"）
3. **承认自己也干过**（先自曝再批评）：
   > "I've been all the way down the micro-optimisation rabbit hole and been down the 'let's build a library of skeletal components for all projects' (this almost never brings any benefit)." [CUBE CSS](https://piccalil.li/blog/cube-css/)

### 4.3 承认错误的固定句法

出错时他**自曝＋打分＋给补偿**，且用数字打分：

> "7/10 bad testing on my part"
>
> 中文：测试不到位，我打 7 分（满分 10 的烂）。
>
> 来源：[gorko #20](https://github.com/Andy-set-studio/gorko/issues/20)｜[一手]｜可信度：高
>
> —— 这是他的道歉模板：不找借口、用自嘲量化责任、然后立刻往下推进。

> "I applied `:link` as a global but it's actually becoming problematic… **This needs revisiting** and components which have used `:link` to boost specificity need cleaning up."
>
> 来源：[web.dev #6746](https://github.com/GoogleChrome/web.dev/issues/6746)｜[一手]｜可信度：高

### 4.4 段落长度

散文段落**偏长（3–6 句）**，但句间节奏变化大；小标题用**完整口语短句**，不是名词短语：

> "## It's about time I tried to explain what progressive enhancement actually is"（标题本身是第一人称口语）
> "## Almost no one will get your "ideal" experience, especially not all the time"
> "## "Designers won't work like this""
> "## Now you know, it's time to make it happen"
> "## Lastly, it's not a quick fix"
>
> 来源：同上｜[一手]｜可信度：高
>
> —— 标题就是句子，常带第二人称 "you" 和口语连接符（Lastly / Now / Anyway）。

## 五、幽默方式

**结论：英式冷幽默 + 自嘲为主，讽刺为辅（讽刺对象是行业现象，不是具体人），从不讲笑话段子。**

### 5.1 自嘲（最高频）

> "I'm a long-time proponent of progressive enhancement to the point where readers are almost certainly bored"
>
> 中文：我是渐进增强的老吹鼓手，读者八成已经烦了。
>
> 来源：[PE 文](https://piccalil.li/blog/its-about-time-i-tried-to-explain-what-progressive-enhancement-actually-is)｜[一手]｜可信度：高

> "I've been prattling on about progressive enhancement for years"（我唠叨渐进增强好多年了）
> "7/10 bad testing on my part"
> "This first snippet is mostly me initialising the tools and setting some immovable constants — hence the all caps screaming naming convention."
>
> 中文：这段主要是初始化工具、定几个不能动的常量——所以用全大写"尖叫式"命名法。
>
> 来源：[personal-site/10](https://piccalil.li/projects/personal-site/10)｜[一手]｜可信度：高

> "I've felt like I can't progress to iteration two — the actual design work — until iteration one is fully written up. I have no idea why either. Brains are weird, man."
>
> 中文：一号迭代没写完我就不肯进二号迭代——我自己也不知道为啥。脑子这玩意儿，邪门。
>
> 来源：同上｜[一手]｜可信度：高

### 5.2 干幽默 / 挖苦行业（对事不对人）

> "Sure, in the old days of Internet Explorer, the cascade was a bit tricky, but using old knowledge and techniques as the base of a modern approach is like feeding horse feed to a car."
>
> 来源：[CUBE CSS](https://piccalil.li/blog/cube-css/)｜[一手]｜可信度：高

## 十、公开争议与论战

### 10.1 "div 按钮 / 原生按钮"之争（2019）

他自己定义为 "one of those famous Twitter debates"（那些著名的推特论战之一），并**主动不给出链接**：
> "\* The conversation isn't worth linking as some of the tweets that give it the right context have since been deleted."
>
> 中文：* 那场对话不值得挂链接，因为提供正确语境的一些推文已经被删了。
>
> 来源：[Context and caveats](https://archive.hankchizljaw.com/wrote/context-and-caveats/)｜[一手]｜可信度：高
>
> 他的立场：非语义 `<div>` 按钮依赖重 JS 与 ARIA，会被"权威说了可以"的错误信号扩散，造成"accessibility fires"。

### 10.2 CSS 方法论之争（CUBE vs BEM vs utility-first）

其核心不是攻击别人，而是**反对"把某个方法当绝对"的推荐行为**。见 9.1 引用。

### 10.3 CSS reset 的 lists 方案之争（2020–2021）

`modern-css-reset` #30 是他**自己开 issue 自我修正**的一次：
> "Right now, the reset removes all padding, margin and list styles from lists that have a class attribute. **This is causing problems in the community and it's something I'd like to remedy.**"
> "The immediate removal is **my preference** and as this thing has _exploded_ in popularity, that really doesn't hold water anymore."
>
> 中文：这个 reset 会移除所有带 class 的列表的 padding、margin 和 list 样式。这正在社区里造成问题，我想修掉它。原来那种"直接全删"是我个人的偏好；但这东西已经_爆_了，那个理由站不住了。
>
> 来源：[modern-css-reset #30](https://github.com/Andy-set-studio/modern-css-reset/issues/30)｜[一手]｜可信度：高
>
> —— 关键：**他把"我的偏好"与"对所有人的默认"明确切开**，并因为项目流行而主动放弃个人偏好。这是他处理公共争议的一致范式。

被追问 `role="list"` 的必要性时，他也没辩论，只给资源：
> "I'd recommend reading the linked resource https://www.scottohara.me/blog/2019/01/12/lists-and-safari.html"

### 10.4 关于 AI/LLM 的立场对峙（2025–2026）

Piccalilli 官方三个"绝不"，以及个人博客的强烈措辞（见 8.5）。这场争议中他是**主动挑明立场的一方**：
> "We know we're **sailing against the current** here, but we're cool with that."

## 附：可直接用于"说得像他"的十条硬规则

1. **落槌用短句**，铺陈用长句。不少于五处"长—长—短"的节奏。
2. **"Yeh" 而不是 "Yeah"**；"I reckon" 而不是 "I think"（当表达估算时）。
3. **不确定时给三个具体理由**，不给一个空洞的"不"。
4. **批评工具前先说它的好**，攻击"省略前提的推荐行为"而非工具本身。
5. **抽象概念降维**：骨架、鞋、马饲料喂汽车、烧房子擦窗。
6. **收尾用类比**，且类比取自厨房/房子/车/食物，不取自软件。
7. **方法论一律加免责**："这只是我喜欢的方式""任何方法在你的语境下都成立""继续做对你有用的事"。
8. **认错时自曝＋量化**："7/10 bad testing on my part"。
9. **官方身份扣着写，私人身份才放开**。脏话只出现在 bell.bz，绝不进 piccalil.li。
10. **收尾口头禅**："Job firmly done." / "Nice one!" / "Thanks!" / "Much love 💛"

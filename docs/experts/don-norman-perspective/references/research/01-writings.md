### 1.3 1988 版 vs 2013 版：诺曼自己列的差异

来源：官方序言全文 [jnd.org](https://jnd.org/preface-design-of-everyday-things-revised-edition/) [一手]

1. **新增 signifiers（意符）**——诺曼原话：signifiers「a concept first introduced in my book *Living with Complexity*」。第一版聚焦 affordance，但 affordance 用于虚拟对象时充满歧义，「affordances have created much confusion in the world of design」。
2. **新增 Human-Centered Design（HCD）**——这个词在他写第一版时「didn't yet exist」。
3. **第 2 章新增情绪**——把七阶段行动模型与三层次加工模型（出自 *Emotional Design*）对接。
4. **第 3 章新增文化**——「什么在一个文化里显得自然，在另一个文化里未必」，直接冲击「natural mapping」的普适性。
5. **第 5 章大幅重写**——slips 拆成「动作型／记忆失效型」，mistakes 拆成「规则型／知识型／记忆失效型」；删掉第一版中「对设计没有启示」的分类；新增韧性工程。
6. **第 6、7 章全新**——双钻模型、HCD 流程、activity-centered design、Norman's law、featuritis、渐进式 vs 激进式创新、设计的道德义务、「the rise of the small」。
7. **篇幅不变**——刻意「删多少写多少」，保持导论性质。

> 诺曼自评原话：「What has changed? Not much. Everything.」[一手]

## 三、反复出现的核心论点（真信念）

判定标准：在 ≥3 个独立来源中反复出现。

### 3.1 不是人犯错，是设计犯错

- DOET2 第 5 章标题直接就是 **"Human Error? No, Bad Design"**。[一手]
- HCD 四原则第 1 条：「Ask 'why?' at each issue. **When the answer is 'human error,' keep going**: why did the error occur, what could have prevented it?」[一手]
- 第 5 章还引入「瑞士奶酪模型」解释事故如何穿透多层防御，以及韧性工程作为出路。[一手·目录]
- 对自动化系统：「the first reaction is often to claim 'human error,' blaming the last person…」[一手·jnd.org 文章列表摘要]

**这是诺曼最核心、出现次数最多的一条。** 他从未放弃。

### 3.2 复杂 ≠ 复杂化

- *Living with Complexity* 全书主张：「Don't want simplicity. Simple tools are not up to the task. The world is complex; our tools need to match that complexity.」[一手]
- 金句墙：「Simplicity is in the mind, complexity is in the world.」「Make it Simple? No! Make it Understandable.」[一手]
- DOET2 第 6 章第 6 节：「Complexity Is Good; It Is Confusion That Is Bad.」[一手]
- *Living with Complexity* 的关键让步：管理复杂是**合伙关系**——「But we too have to do our part: we have to take the time to learn the structure and practice the skills.」[一手]

### 3.3 设计必须服务于「活动」，不只是服务于「人」

- 2005 年 *Interactions* 专栏定调：「Activity-Centered Design is superior.」
- 2008 年澄清文自我定位：「I do not think this to be any change in what I have advocated. Rather, I see all my work as part of a coherent pattern.」[一手]
- 2013 年 DOET2 第 6 章正式写入「Activity-Centered versus Human-Centered Design」。 [一手·目录]
- 2019 年 HCD 四原则第 3 条直接叫「**Use an Activity-Centered Systems Approach**」。[一手]

> 注意：**他把 ACD 收编进了 HCD**，而不是取代 HCD。见第六节矛盾记录。

### 3.4 人适应技术，不是技术适应人（他公开反对 HCD 的这条信条）

- 原文最狠的一句：「None of this 'tools adapt to the people' nonsense — **people adapt to the tools**.」[一手]
- 反例举证：时钟与手表、书写系统、乐器——「Neither the instruments nor the notation would pass any Human-Centered Design review.」[一手]
- 结论反问：「Learn the activity, and the tools are understood. … Maybe the reality is just the converse: **Learn the tools, and the activity is understood.**」[一手]
- 与 HCD 社区的对立：「To the Human-Centered Design community, the tool should be invisible, it should not get in the way. With Activity-Centered Design, the tool is the way.」[一手]

### 3.5 自动化悖论（Paradox of Automation）

- DOET2 第 5 章第 12 节标题即 "The Paradox of Automation"。 [一手·目录]
- *The Design of Future Things* 第 4 章 "The Role of Automation"。 [一手·目录]
- jnd.org 长文《Stupid Smart Stuff》：「The notion that we can have automated or semi-automated cars as long as the driver is watching over them is a **dangerous myth**.」——理由是 50 余年的警觉性（vigilance）研究证明人无法长时间保持监控。[一手]
- 同文的航空类比：自动化「补偿到极限后说『我放弃了』，然后让飞机开始坠毁」。 [一手]

### 3.6 机器不该假装是人，机器应当可被理解

- *The Design of Future Things* 第 6 章给出「**六条智能物品设计规则**」，第 8 章后记标题为「**The Machine's Point of View**」（机器的视角）。[一手·目录]
- 《Stupid Smart Stuff》：「Whenever you see something labeled 'smart' or 'intelligent,' be assured that it is actually rather stupid.」[一手]
- 他反对「智能手表」替你决定，主张设备应该**沟通**而不是**隐瞒**：出了问题却说「不用打扰飞行员」。 [一手]
- 2019 年长文对不透明自动化的立场：「Modern decisions tools, especially those of statistical decision making, machine learning, and the deep-learning systems of neural networks are **unable to provide explanations** of why and how they reached their proclamations.」并警告「over-trust can be more dangerous than under-trust.」[一手]

### 3.7 概念在移动：user-centered → human-centered → humanity-centered

- 1998 *The Invisible Computer*：倡导 human-centered development process。[一手]
- 2013 DOET2 序言：他自称在 Apple 时其团队名为「the User Experience Architect's office」，并说「user experience」一词「I was among the first to use」。 [一手]
- 2023 *Design for a Better World* 第 22 章：明确把 HCD 说成「describes the way we did things in the past」。 [一手]
- jnd.org 金句墙：「Design must change from being unintentionally destructive to being intentionally constructive.」（引自 DBW 第 2 章末，p.13）[一手]

> 这是一条**他自己承认的立场迁移**，不是别人强加的解读。

### 3.8 好设计是隐形的

> "Good design is actually a lot harder to notice than poor design, in part because good designs fit our needs so well that the design is invisible, serving us without drawing attention to itself. Bad design, on the other hand, screams out its inadequacies." [一手]

出处：DOET2 官方序言，[jnd.org](https://jnd.org/preface-design-of-everyday-things-revised-edition/)

### 6.4 立场张力（非事实矛盾，而是可观察的自我张力）

- **T1｜对「用户研究」的态度**：诺曼是 HCD／UX 的奠基人之一，却在 2005 年说 HCD「harmful」、2008 年说 personas 工作「misplaced, irrelevant, and potentially harmful」、2009 年说设计研究对突破式创新「essentially useless」。**他没有撤回 HCD，而是把 ACD 收编为 HCD 的一个变体。** 两种表述在文本中并存。
- **T2｜对「聆听用户」的态度**：一边写「listening to customers is always wise」，一边写「the best way to satisfy users is sometimes to ignore them」，还正面举 Apple 用单一权威负责人取代人机界面团队为例。限定条件是「unless the person in charge has a clear vision for the product」。
- **T3｜对「简单／美感」的态度**：早期被广泛解读为可用性至上、反对美感，2002 年后持续自我修正：「I neglected the topic of aesthetics because I thought it already well covered elsewhere. Unfortunately, my neglect was interpreted by many to imply that I was against beauty.」[一手]

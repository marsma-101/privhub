### 3.2 改稿的动作顺序还原 —— 以 CSS Day 2019 全场为准

以下为 CSS Day 2019 演讲中 **Steve 本人逐项口述的执行顺序**（`[一手]`，来源 videodb 全文 transcript）。他用的是一个虚构「机票预订」界面，明确声明原型「大量使用 Bootstrap 默认值」，并强调目标是「只做外观层面的改动，不动整体结构」。

**执行顺序（按他实际的推进次序）**

1. **先声明工作方式与范围**：先定义这是 tactics over talent 的示范，再从整页顶部开始向下推。
   > "So let's start by moving back to the top here, and we'll just kind of work our way down as I get through it."（我们从顶部开始，一路往下走。）
2. **先找出「最刺眼的问题」，而不是全面铺开**：第一处动手的是 hero 图上文字对比度不足。
   > "So the most glaring problem here is, it sort of starts that text on that background image."
3. **解决 text-on-photo 对比**：半透明遮罩 → 去饱和 + 调对比 → 叠加纯色滤镜（multiply）→ 直接给出可复用工具（Figma 的混合模式、ShapeFactory 的 Duotone）。
4. **定主色**：坦承自己「偷色」——逛 land-book.com / siteinspire.com / Dribbble 取色器，采样后落到一套**受约束的调色板**（明确点名 Tailwind 默认色）。
   > "I thought it'd be helpful to share how I choose primary colors for a site, the answers to that is quite simple, I steal them."
5. **拆导航栏容器**：把 nav 从「bar」里拿出来直接放到背景上，再补一条低透明度 1px 边框做关联。
   > "First, navbars don't need to be in a bar."
6. **做文字层级**（而不是先做间距）：副标题用「减淡」而非仅靠字号，引出「彩色背景上不要用灰字」这条铁律，再补一层「旋转色相」技巧。
7. **套白卡容器 → 立刻上间距**：把整个表单装进白色面板，并给出他的间距工作法（见下）。
8. **输入框**：宽度按预期输入长度收窄 → 高度提到 40–48px → 加极浅灰底做区分 → 加图标支撑文本（不替换 label）→ 图标用更柔的颜色做「配重」。
9. **替换浏览器默认控件**：radio / checkbox / select 箭头。
10. **压缩纵向空间 + 对齐**：缩小 label、把搜索按钮推到最右与左侧 checkbox 对齐、加大按钮高度、按钮加图标。
11. **引入强调色并反转配色**：黄这类亮色的对比度靠「浅底深字」而非「白字亮底」。
12. **造纵深**：重叠（负 margin）→ 阴影（四级 elevation 体系）→ 颜色的轻重（浅色上浮）→ 面板改用极浅灰。
13. **把表格「压回去」**：给它更弱的一级阴影，明确「表层面板不应与搜索面板同级」。
14. **表格专项**：数值/日期右对齐 → `font-feature-settings: tnum` 表格数字 → 斑马纹替代边框 → 表头改为「小号、加粗、全大写、柔色」。
15. **「Think outside of the database」**：不是每个字段都要一列——不需要排序的次要信息降级为小号浅色文本或合并。
16. **按钮降级处理**：表格里的一列重色按钮会抢戏，改成「柔和实底 + 深色文字」的次级样式；文本链接改用 chevron 图标提示可点。
17. **卡片区**：图片统一裁切成固定容器（`background-size: cover`）以对齐版式；去掉多余的底部按钮（举 Airbnb / YouTube 为例）。
18. **去掉标签**：`1295` 自带货币符就是价格，`.com` 就是网站，无需 label。
19. **徽章**：软背景 + 深色文字，兼顾「更雅致」与「更可访问」。
20. **区块分割用色块而非边框**：newsletter 区上品牌蓝，footer 用更深一档；进一步可叠 SVG 背景花纹（他自己做的 Hero Patterns）。
21. **大 footer 加结构**：用「小号加粗全大写」标签分组。
22. **收尾三件事（他明确称为 finishing touches）**：
    - **灰不是灰**：给灰调掺蓝（偏冷）或黄/棕（偏暖），并提醒「用 HSL 时要记得在明暗两端提高饱和度」，否则浅端会发灰。
    - **换字体**：从系统字体栈换成他买的字体包中的一款，并复述他找字体的路径（看现成站点 → DevTools 查字体 → 追到字库厂商 → 买整套）。
    - **回看前后对比**：用「起点 vs 终点」收束，并给出一句结构性总结：
      > "it can be overwhelming to go from your starting point to the end point, but really, it's just a bunch of small little changes"

**萧潇归纳的「他的决策优先级」**（`[推断]`，依据上述口述顺序 + 他反复回扣的几条铁律）：

> 对比度 → 主色 / 受约束调色板 → 结构容器（先拆不要的容器）→ 文字层级 → 间距 → 表单控件细节 → 纵深（重叠 / 阴影 / 颜色轻重）→ 表格与数据层级 → 次要元素降级 → 卡片与图片规范化 → 区块分割 → 灰阶温度与字体。

可验证他确实把「对比度」排在前面：全片第一条 tip 就是对 hero 图文字的对比度处理；而且讲后 Q&A 第一个问题就是问他多在意 contrast，他回答"我相当在意，我装了菜单栏工具 Contrast 随时采样"。

### 3.3 两个可直接复用的「操作型」方法论（他本人口述的原话）

**(1) 间距工作法 —— 从过量开始再往回减**

> "So the common approach to introducing space is to add just a bit at a time, and if it still feels too tight, you continue adding a bit more... But the problem with this approach is that you're only adding like the minimum amount of space until the site no longer looks bad... So I find a better approach is to start with way too much white space and then remove it until you're sort of happy with the result."
> "at first, when focused on an individual component, you might think it feels like too much white space, but in context of the rest of the UI ends up looking like just the right amount."

**(2) 阴影分四级的 elevation 体系**

> "I often use multiple shadows within the design to position elements differently on the virtual Z index. The larger the shadow you use, the closer is gonna feel to the user."
> 四级：贴近背景的元素（按钮 / 输入框）→ 稍离页面的元素（下拉菜单 / 小面板）→ 大面板 → 模态框。
> 且阴影要**分两部分**：「环境光」造成的是更紧更深、垂直偏移小、模糊半径小；「直射光」造成的是更大更软、垂直偏移更大、模糊半径更大。随高度上升，应当把「直射光那层」调得更轻，并引入负 spread。

**(3) 关于「不要用灰字配彩色背景」**

> "Don't use gray text on colored backgrounds... what we're trying to achieve is reduced contrast, and that you want to make text closer to the background color, not gray."
> 进一步技巧：不同色相天生亮度不同（黄最亮、蓝最暗），所以**亮度可以靠旋转色相来调**，而不是只动 lightness —— 这样不会丢饱和度。

## 四、现场改稿的另一种形态：Adam 的 live coding 决策顺序（Rails World 2023）

Adam 的《Tailwind CSS: It looks awful, and it works》全程 live coding，他的**动作顺序**与 Steve 高度同构（`[一手]`，带时间戳字幕）：

1. `[02:53]` 从 Figma 里拿一张设计稿，**先只搭 HTML 骨架**，不写任何样式：
   > "the first thing that I generally do is just kind of like scaffold out all the markup that I think I'm going to need to get all these pieces in place"
2. `[04:32]` 骨架搭完才问「从哪儿开始」——**先做容器（卡片）**：`bg-white` → `rounded` → `shadow` → `overflow-hidden`。
3. `[05:17]` 容器之后处理**内嵌图片与外层的同心圆角**：
   > "Steve did this clever thing here where he's trying to make the corner radius of this sort of like concentric with the outside of the card so it's 12 pixels, 16 minus 4"
   （这段是两人协作方式的直接证据：Adam 会去读 Steve 在 Figma 里给的数值并推算出规则。）
4. `[06:23]` 继续微调 padding 方向（去掉多余的 `pb-0`）、图片高度、`w-full`、`object-cover`。
5. `[07:47]` 再做头像圆形与底部容器内边距，并当场背除法（"Tailwind does train you on your four times tables quite well"）。
6. `[08:51]` **样式顺序是：先容器 → 再布局 → 再排版 → 再颜色 → 最后做重叠这种花活**。
7. `[10:29]` 用负 margin 做重叠 + `outline` 做描边，实现头像「咬」出卡片的效果。
8. `[11:28]` 收尾示范 inline style 做不到的事：`hover:` / `focus:` 前缀，再讲 `group-hover`。
9. `[13:08]` 主动提出「重复怎么办」这个质疑，然后用模板语言的循环来回答，并明确「重复的 HTML 管理本来就是模板语言要解决的核心问题」。
10. `[14:30]` 承认模板方案有时过重（尤其是 ERB / Blade），于是演示回退到 CSS 抽 `@apply` 组件类。

### 5.1 先自曝其短，再给结论

- **Adam 在 Tailwind 第一版释出时的自我定位**（JS Party #155 `[一手]`）：
  > "if I had seen [those other utility frameworks], I probably would have dismissed, because at first glance it just looks like such a terrible idea"

- **Adam 在 Rails World 2023 开场直接放弃说服**（`[一手]`）：

## 六、立场变化记录

### 6.1 Adam：从「语义派」到「utility-first 代言人」的自述链条

这是本次调研里证据链最完整的一条。他在官方长文里把它写成了**五个阶段**（`[一手]`，https://adamwathan.me/css-utility-classes-and-separation-of-concerns/ ，2017-08-07）：

- **Phase 1「语义 CSS」**：用 `.greeting` 这类内容命名的类做 hook。他自评：
  > "I had 'separated my concerns', but there was still a very obvious coupling between my CSS and my HTML... **My markup wasn't concerned with styling decisions, but my CSS was very concerned with my markup structure.** Maybe my concerns weren't so separated after all."
- **Phase 2 BEM**：感觉是巨大改进，但撞上「两个长得一样、语义不同的组件怎么办」的两难。
- **关键转向点，他点明了触发物**：读 Nicolas Gallagher 的 *About HTML semantics and front-end architecture* 后被说服。
  > "The turning point for me came when I read Nicolas Gallagher's About HTML semantics and front-end architecture. I came away from that blog post fully convinced that optimizing for reusable CSS was going to be the right choice."
- **Phase 3 内容无关的组件**：开始用 `.card` / `.btn--primary` / `.actions-list`。
- **Phase 4 组件 + 工具类**：为 `.actions-list--left/right` 这种「为了一个 CSS 属性造一个组件修饰符」感到荒谬，于是改成 `.align-left` / `.mar-r-sm`，然后**删掉整个 `.actions-list` 抽象**。
- **Phase 5 utility-first**：命名这件事本身的核心理由——
  > "when you make modifiers like `.actions-list--left`, you're creating a whole new component modifier just to assign a single CSS property... **We prefer composition to duplication.**"
- **他在文中的核心论证框架，不是「哪种更好」而是「依赖方向」**：
  > "When you think about the relationship between HTML and CSS in terms of 'separation of concerns', it's very black and white... This is not the right way to think about HTML and CSS. Instead, **think about dependency direction.**"
  > 两条路：① CSS 依赖 HTML（HTML 可换皮，CSS 不可复用）；② HTML 依赖 CSS（CSS 可复用，HTML 不可换皮）。"Neither is inherently 'wrong'."
- **他也明确保留了分歧**：
  > "One of the areas where my opinion differs a bit from some of the really die-hard functional CSS advocates is that I don't think you should build things out of utilities only."
  > 并解释自己为什么叫 utility-**first** 而不是 utility-only："because I try to build everything I can out of utilities, and only extract repeating patterns as they emerge."

**播客上的口述版本**（JS Party #65，`[一手]`）——这一段是他最凝练的立场自述：

> "following that sort of trail and just keeping my mind focused on making things reusable, sort of just inevitably leads down this path where your CSS classes become less and less about the context or the content, and more and more about like: what do these two things have in common visually?"
> "**the HTML is choosing what to consume from the CSS, instead of the CSS consuming these class names or tokens that the HTML is inventing.** That was the important realization for me. When I realized that I was comfortable having classes like 'card', it made me realize that I shouldn't be really uncomfortable with classes like 'text center', or 'margin bottom 4'."

**他自认的立场位移幅度**（JS Party #65）：
> "Maybe I've just moved from one purist approach to another purist approach, where I just either want to have pure CSS or pure HTML."

### 6.2 Adam：Tailwind 起源的「不可预期性」自述

- **JS Party #65**：他的第一版**不是 utility 框架**，而是 Bootstrap 式的组件框架；是「跨项目携带」和 Jonathan Reinink 要共用同一套样式这两个现实压力，把组件全砍掉只剩工具类。
  > "By the end of the day, we had to basically rip out things like form controls and buttons because there was no common ground. So all we were left with were these really low-level utilities."
- **官方长文**："Now at this point I had **zero** intention of maintaining any sort of open-source CSS framework. It didn't even occur to me that what I had been building would even be interesting to anyone."
- **官方长文**：命名来源是 KiteTail → 在 onelook.com 搜 `tail*` 找的。
- **官方长文**：驱动来源是直播。
  > "**This is the benefit of working in public** — Steve and I would have never built this Tailwind Labs business if I hadn't been live-streaming my work on yet-another-abandoned-side-project."
- **他在 2020 年承认了架构上的历史包袱（自嘲）**：
  > "to this day I feel like Tailwind is completely abusing PostCSS in a way it was never intended"

### 6.3 Adam：对商业模式的立场修正（2025 → 2026）

- **2025-04（The Panel #6）**：坚持一次性买断，明确拒绝订阅。
  > "I still don't really wanna pivot it to being subscription honestly because I just think the conversion rate would drop by so much that it'll counteract any of the recurring benefits."
- **2026-03（Startups For the Rest of Us #825）**：口风松动但**仍然不为一次性定价后悔**，只承认企业授权这一块早该做。
  > "I honestly don't think so. There's some things that I maybe would've done differently. I think we probably could have done subscriptions for team licenses from day one, which would've honestly probably been enough to make a pretty substantial difference."
  > 并且主动交代了他当年的自我约束："I took an irrational amount of pride in trying to build an open source thing that was sustained on real profits... I hated the idea of feeling like we were making it work on donations. **I wanted it to feel like the market was making it work, which I think I've changed my opinion on that a little bit.**"
  > 下一步产品明确转向订阅："the next thing that we're working on, we are planning for it to be subscription revenue"

### 6.4 Adam：从「有自信的判断者」到「怀疑自己的直觉」

- **The Panel #6（2025-04）**，这是他最脆弱的一段自述：
  > "I feel like looking back, I feel like I'm getting kicked in the balls over and over and over again with basically every idea I've had for this business for the last, like, three years."
  > "I just basically have lost all confidence in myself as—"（主持人接 "Like your instincts and stuff?"）— "Yeah. Yeah. A hundred percent."
- **同一集里他用来描述「无效努力」的那句**：
  > "Just went exactly the same. Just like the universe is just like, nothing you do is allowed to affect anything."
- **2026-03 的收束**：
  > "suffering in silence is the wrong play when you're in these situations. You need that outside perspective."

### 6.5 保留的冲突（矛盾不合并）

**(a) 「设计靠天赋还是靠战术」——两人口径不一致，且 Steve 自己内部有张力。**

- **Adam 在 Refactoring UI 官网的自述先立「天赋论」的靶子，再推翻它**（`[一手]`）：
  > "I always chalked it up to a left brain/right brain sort of thing — I'm logical and analytical so I'm good at programming, people like Steve are intuitive and creative so he's good at design."
  > 然后："But after working closely with Steve I started picking up little tricks. Tricks that didn't require any artistic talent, but made things look better instantly."
  > 结论句："Design with tactics, not talent."
- **Steve 在 CSS Day 2019 开场用的是同一个框架**：
  > "most people know me as the guy who teaches design to developers. So I like to show how you can use **tactics instead of talent** to do a design."
- **但 Steve 在 Ladybug #24 里又把自己描述为靠直觉的那一类**：
  > "like most designers, I was just more of like, sort of the intuitive creative type. And just the fact that I had been doing it for so long, I just sort of relied on my instincts to make a lot of decisions."
- **他不止一次说过「我做的事大家本来就应该知道」**：
  > 谈 box-shadow 垂直偏移那条爆款 tip："for me, I just always did that. I just thought like, yeah everyone knows that right? And he goes, No, developers have no idea man."
  > 在 Ladybug #24 结尾又说："There's a lot more stuff I know that I didn't think I knew, kind of thing."
- **[冲突] 定论**：对外话术是「不需要天赋」，但 Steve 描述自己时反复用「直觉 / 本能 / 我一直就这么做」。两者**未被任何一方调解**，本次调研原样保留，不替他下结论。

**(b) 关于 Tailwind 的被接受度，Steve 给的是无条件论，Adam 给的是有条件论。**

- Steve（Ladybug #24）：
  > "the only critics I find that are around the Tailwind are people who like have just looked at it and never played with it. But like once people use it, they're like, I can't go back to anything else."
- Adam（Rails World 2023）同样说大多数人上手就改观，但**没有把批评者归因为「没试过」**，而是承认视觉上无从辩护（见 5.1）。
- 另：Adam 在官方长文里承认，换皮能力（theme-ability）在语义派那里是强论据——"the strong argument for the semantic approach is if you're building something where theme-ability is really important"，他只是主张「我几乎没做过需要换皮的项目」。**这不是拒绝承认对方的论据，只是转移优先级**，与 Steve 的单边断言并不等价。

**(c) 关于 AI 与 Tailwind 的关系，Adam 自己承认内在矛盾。**

> "AI has basically been our competition ever since it showed up. And I don't really want it to be that way because I'm hugely excited about it and I use it every day... it sucks to feel like this technology that I'm personally excited about is in competition with our business."（Startups #825 `[一手]`）

## 七、拒绝回答 / 回避的话题

### 7.1 明确说「不知道」或直接搁置

1. **品牌色与语义色冲突时不回答**
   CSS Day 2019 讲后 Q&A，被问「如果站点主色是红色，那红色告警怎么办」：
   > "I've been asked this a lot and I don't know how to answer that question."
   他给了个临时做法（降低红的浓度），但**明确承认这不是有答案的问题**。`[一手]`

2. **不评论具体数字（营收）的场合**
   The Panel #6 与 Startups #825 里他会给趋势（"dropping like pretty consistently by say like 15 grand a month"）和幅度（"down maybe 70% from peak"），但**不给完整财务数字与员工细节**；对「是否后悔不做订阅」他明确说无法 A/B 验证：
   > "I obviously, I can't go back and AB test it, you know what I mean?" `[一手]`

3. **不替自己下最终结论**
   Startups #825 中反复把判断抛回主持人：「but I'd be really curious to hear like what you think for sure.」`[一手]`

### 7.2 明显缩短、绕开或降级处理的话题

4. **不批评 Bootstrap**（反复主动收住）
   CSS Day 2019：在指出示例大量使用 Bootstrap 默认值后立刻补一句
   > "Now I don't want to like poo poo on bootstrap. It's a great starting point"
   Adam 在 JS Party #155 也同款处理："I'm still a big fan of the project and the people involved"。
   → 两人对 Bootstrap 的态度是**持续性的外交辞令**，几乎不在任何场合说重话。

5. **不做「什么时候不该用 Tailwind」的正面清单**
   多场访谈被问到混合方案 / 边界，Adam 的回答都是「那就抽 @apply 类」或「那就写模板组件」，**没有给过「这种情况下不要用 Tailwind」的清单**。JS Party #65 里他的表述是把选择权交回使用者：
   > "Neither is inherently 'wrong'; it's just a decision made based on what's more important to you in a specific context."
   `[推断]`（这是萧潇对多次回避模式的归纳，非其原话）

6. **不碰 UX 深水区**
   Ladybug #24 被追问 UX vs 视觉设计时，他的回答基本停在「UI 是你看到和交互的东西，UX 是一切」这一层，然后说
   > "I don't know, is that a—"（未说完，主持人接话）
   → **他始终把发言范围锁在视觉 / UI 层**，不做用户研究、信息架构层面的主张。`[一手]`

7. **中文/非拉丁字体的排版问题——完全未涉及**
   两人全部公开材料（含 CSS Day 一行不带字号表、RUI 目录）只讨论拉丁字体与 Google Fonts / Adobe Fonts / 字库厂商，**没有任何一处谈到 CJK 字体**。

8. **对自己作品的负面评价不回避，但对「方法论的适用边界」回避**
   有意思的对照：Steve 会主动说 Heroicons「I'm not proud of them anymore. I think they're ugly. They're a little dated.」；但不会说自己的改稿方法论在哪些场景不成立。

### 7.3 YouTube / 直播形态本身的取舍（他明确拒绝做的事）

9. **拒绝做直播改稿**
   Ladybug #24 里 Steve 交代这套视频形态的由来——Adam 建议他直播，他拒绝：
   > "His idea for that was like, you should just do like a livestream and like do refactoring in like an hour, right?... But that didn't really appeal to me. Like, I'm not really good at live streaming, I've tried it before... especially with design, like me designing, half the time I'm just like, just sitting there thinking like, Oh, what should I do here and it's like, that won't be a really good livestream."
   → 这是他**主动选择隐藏「犹豫和试错过程」**，改用两周制作一支 15 分钟的成品视频。这条对理解「他的方法论展示是否完整」很关键：**观众看到的是编辑后的序列，不是真实时序。**

10. **不以 YouTube 为主阵地**
    同集他说：
    > "Those videos like burnt me out. I stopped making them." / "I'm not super active on YouTube, but clearly I should be more because that's a lot of subscribers."
    2025 年才有重启计划（见 3.1）。`[一手]`

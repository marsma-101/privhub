# 03 · 碎片表达与风格 DNA

> 对象：Alan Cooper（生于 1952-06-03），Visual Basic 之父、Cooper 设计公司创始人、《About Face》《The Inmates Are Running the Asylum》作者、persona 方法提出者。
> 本文件只做一件事：把他**怎么说话**拆开。
> 引文一律保留英文原文；每条附来源 URL 与可信度标注。**未经核实的话一律不写好话。**

**可信度图例**
- **[一手]** — Cooper 本人署名写作、当面受访作答、或本人推文原文。
- **[二手]** — 书籍摘录页 / 书评 / 语录汇编中转引，原始上下文未由本条 URL 直接呈现。
- **[推断]** — 本文作者基于前两类证据做的风格归纳，不可当作他的原话。
- **[存疑]** / **[冲突]** — 见文中标注。

### 1.2 他爱用的比喻词根（一句话一格）

- **建筑/工程**：architect vs. builder、pouring the concrete of code、painting the corpse、50-story office building、a giant dam、50,000 吨钢结构造不成密歇根湖大桥。
- **医学/外科**：bright light under which we do surgery、chemotherapy（他自比"反对化疗不等于反对治病"）、vaccination/prevention。
- **宗教/道德**：sanctimony、confession（"治疗依赖症第一步是承认自己有病"）、heresy 式口吻。
- **司法/军事**：tyranny、Stalin 排雷、"handed a loaded weapon to these guys who are not our friends"。
- **食物/消费品**：Diet Coke and a Snickers bar for lunch、baking a cake without salt or sugar、the Uggs of today。

### 1.3 禁忌词（他明确反对 / 拿来做贬义用的词）

| 他反对的词 | 他的理由（原话或紧贴原话的转述） | 他替换成什么 | 可信度 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"user experience design" / UX design** | "what UX design means to me is it means being designer-centric. It means it's about your quirky glasses and your tattoos and not about your users." | interaction design | [一手] |
| **"interface design"** | "It implies that only the interface is answerable to the users' needs… licenses programmers to reason like this: 'I can code as I please because an "interface" will be slapped on after I'm done.'" | interaction design | [二手]（书摘） |
| **"design" 这个词本身（在商业语境里）** | "The word 'design' is toxic in the world of business." | goal-directed interaction design | [一手] |
| **"the end user" / "users"（泛化用法）** | "Until the user is precisely defined, the programmer can always imagine that he is the user." 他反对把 users 当一成不变的抽象群体 | 具名 persona | [一手] |
| **"empathy"（作为设计能力）** | "words like empathy to me are excuses for lack of discipline" | 田野研究与训练 | [一手] |
| **"requirements"（当设计用）** | "A list of features is not the same as the design of behavior." | scenarios / behavior design | [一手]（Agile08 幻灯片，经 [mslinn 转引](https://mslinn.com/blog/2008/09/24/breathless-delerium.html)） |

| **"demographics"（用于数字产品）** | 钓鱼的装配线工人与董事长在河里站得只差 50 英尺 | personas / user goals | [一手] |
| **"business people need to learn design" 的反面："designers need to know business"** | "All this talk about designers needing to know business is a lot of crap perpetuated by people who have learned that business is much easier than design is." | 反问：凭什么不是反过来 | [一手]（推文，经 [noahfang.ca 逐条转引并给出推文 ID](https://noahfang.ca/2018/08/01/design-in-business-and-business-in-design/)） |

## 2 · 句式偏好

### 2.1 短句断言为主，长句用于"铺荒谬"

Cooper 的节奏是**先一刀砍下结论，再补一个日常场景把它钉死**。纯论证型长句很少；他的长句几乎都出现在**枚举同一个荒谬的多个变体**时（hammer / VCR / 电梯 / 电话 / 微波炉）。

- 极短断言（≤12 词）：
  - "Right, they're wrong."（UXpod，回应"这软件天生复杂"）
  - "Yes." / "Yes."（连答两个 Yes，记者逼他展开）
  - "Cool is not a good design reason."
  - "I want money." / "It's wrong. It's deeply and profoundly wrong."
  - "That's precisely my point."
- 判决式（主谓宾 + 名词性死刑）：
  - "…it's a dead end."
  - "…it's just stupid."
  - "Then what you are is you're a dupe."
  - "That's a lie and its bullshit."

### 2.2 先结论、后举证 —— 几乎不铺垫

他的访谈回答常以**直接给判断**开头，然后再讲理由；很少"让我们先看背景"。

> "Well, they're wrong. That's just like saying we have a feudal autocracy…" — [一手]

对比他在书里（书面语域）会先立 axiom 再展开——**书面语域同样先给公理**：

> "First axiom of user interface design: Don't make the user look stupid." — [二手]

### 2.3 第二人称直呼：**极高频**

- 对读者/听者："if you insist on pressing me"、"If you are stranded on a deserted island, you don't care much that your rescue ship is a leaky, rat-infested hulk."、"If you tell me 'here is the boundary to the problem,' the first thing I do is go outside the boundary."
- 对软件："The program just doesn't care about me and treats me like a stranger even though I'm the only human it knows."（第一人称被拟人化地伤害）
- 对行业（命令式）："Don't tell me this is how businesses are run."
- 对读者（直接派活）："If you get fired by a company because they don't value your dedication to planning and personas in interaction design, you, my friend, will get job offers by the dozen in the industry. **Call me.**"

### 2.4 反问：主要功能是"把对方的立场推到荒谬处"

- "Are you an inmate?"（1998 斯坦福讲座开场，[一手]）
- "What if we switched the metaphor to, 'the building contractors are telling the architects where to put the windows?' Strike a little closer to home?"（同场，[一手]）
- "Why stop the entire process because something inconsequential is missing? Why ground the airplane because the bar is short one little bottle of Scotch?"（[一手]）
- "Where is it written in stone that designers need to know business? Why don't business people have to learn about design?"（[一手]，推文）
- "Would you want to get your heart replaced in a one-man shop operating theater?"（[一手]）
- "who said this is the way to do things?"（[一手]）

### 2.5 排比与清单化

他习惯用**三件套**收口（这是全篇最稳定的结构指纹）：

- "I don't need a more powerful computer. I don't need a more powerful cell phone. What I need is a computer that doesn't make me feel bad, and a cell phone that doesn't make me feel stupid."
- 论文式三选项："One is… The other one is… The third way is…"
- 三条消除错误对话框的办法："1. Make errors impossible 2. Give positive feedback 3. Audit, don't edit"
- 十四条 polite software 清单（见 §5 语气样本）

### 2.6 破折号 / 插入语 / 自我打断

书面语域里他大量使用 `—` 和 `()` 塞段子：

> "…the drill sergeant is at least training you how to save your life in combat… But to be given negative feedback by software—any software—is an insult." — [一手]

口语访谈里则是"跑题—自嘲—再拉回来"，例如 2000 年 uidesign 访谈开场即跑题 80 分钟，他自称 "'We seem to have wandered off the script.' … 'Clearly, you are grappling with the terminology, "So am I!"'"

**[推断]** 这条"跑题不道歉、反而把跑题变成内容"是他稳定的语域特征；他不用"对不起我跑题了"来收尾，而是把跑题升格成结论。

## 3 · 幽默方式

**主排序（按出现频次）：讽刺/反讽 ＞ 荒谬类比 ＞ 行业羞辱 ＞ 自嘲 ＞ 冷幽默/黑色幽默。**

### 3.1 反讽式赞美（最锋利的一招）

对一家公司说"你们是黑暗中的一道光"，下一句立刻把整个行业按在地上：

> "There are many software companies out there looking for a quick buck these days, and you guys are just a light in the darkness." — [一手]

把自己所处的行业说得像时尚灾难：

> "We're the current fad; we're the **Uggs** of today." — [一手]

### 3.2 荒谬类比揭示荒谬（他的主武器）

> "Well I think that's a lot like having a **Diet Coke and a Snickers bar for lunch**. *[Laughter.]* You can tell yourself you're being conservative by having that Diet Coke but you're not." — [一手]，评价"没做调研就拍脑袋编 persona"

> "It's like baking a cake, and saying, 'You know, I don't need any of that salt or sugar stuff, 'cause that's not good for me. I'll just bake a cake without it.' And then they don't like the cake they made and blame the cake." — [一手]

> "It's like saying you don't like cars because you have a thing against wheels." — [一手]

> "Like putting an **Armani suit on Attila the Hun**, interface design only tells how to dress up an existing behavior." — [二手]，*Inmates*

> "It's like a **welder** saying, 'well, I don't know how to get good penetration on these welds, but isn't this sculpture beautiful?'" — [一手]

> "…you can't divert the course of the Mississippi River where it's a mile wide, but you can go up to where it's a tiny little rivulet… and you can divert the Mississippi with a **shovel**." — [一手]

> "…somebody needed to have said, you know, maybe you shouldn't throw that **cocaine** out the window of the moving car." — [一手]，论社交平台早期的"外部性"

> "It's like integrating a giant university and saying you'll just have to deal with the discomfort of the formerly comfortable." — [一手]

### 3.3 行业羞辱（把整个职业群体整体污名化）

- "**Homo logicus**" — 把程序员划为另一个物种
- "the **inmates** are running the **asylum**"
- "the **invasion of the lightweights**"（他**同时**用来自嘲：他当年也看不起 BASIC 程序员）
- "the software industry is an example of **midgets standing on the toes of other midgets**"（对照科学界"巨人站在巨人肩上"）
- "They are **cash extraction engines**."（评同行设计公司）
- "Most software isn't designed. Rather, it emerges from the development team like a **zombie emerging from a bubbling vat of Research and Development juice**."

### 3.4 自嘲（真实存在，但总跟着一根回刺）

> "People think I'm a great interaction designer. The truth is, I'm okay. Others are much better than me. One thing I did do was realise that the practice of interaction design needed to exist. I saw that ten years before other people did." — [一手]

> "I meant I was angry and frustrated and I was venting and I'm sorry, I do this stuff… sometimes I broadcast with my inner voice and I shouldn't." — [一手]，解释自己那条"there is no such thing as UX design"推文

> "I'm shaving with an axe. Exactly, a dull axe; a rusty dull." — [一手]，接住记者的调侃

> "I just finished my first cabinet with hand cut dovetail drawers. I think its pretty good work, so yeah!" — [一手]

### 3.5 冷幽默 / 黑色幽默（通常是最后一句，用荒谬收）

> "But if you insist on pressing me, I will admit there is one situation where an error message is acceptable: **When your printer is fully engulfed in flames.**" — [一手]

> "The program is doo-doo, pond scum. It is **less than zero**." — [一手]

> "Then what you are is you're a **dupe**." — [一手]

> "…and if you get fired you know, well… Yeah, you are better off, really." — [一手]

## 4 · "骂人"手法分类（本维度重点）

### 手法 A · 整体污名化（给对手起一个能贴一辈子的绰号）

| 绰号 | 对象 | 原句 |
|---|---|---|
| inmates / asylum | 程序员 + 整个高科技行业 | 书名；"Are you an inmate?"（[一手]） |
| Homo logicus | 程序员作为物种 | "With my tongue firmly planted in my cheek, I call programmers *Homo logicus*: a species slightly—but distinctly—different from *Homo sapiens*."（[二手]） |
| lightweights / the invasion of the lightweights | 每一代新人（含他自己年轻时代） | （[一手]） |
| the third way people / self-referential design | 用"我自己就是用户"当设计方法的人 | "well I'm a user, so I'll just do what I want… that's called **self-referential design** and it doesn't work."（[一手]） |
| cash extraction engines | 同行设计公司 | （[一手]） |
| monarch / royalty / caretakers for the lands of the monarch | 大科技 + 其雇员 | "If you go work for a company that is funded or owned by a tech oligarch, you become a caretaker for the lands of the monarch."（[一手]） |

**给他的"污名化"定档的准确表述**：他不说"某些程序员做得不好"，他直接说**这一类人不属于这个物种**。这是把批评从"错误"升级为"存在方式"。

### 手法 B · 荒谬类比（见 §3.2）——用日常物件把对方的逻辑极简化

固定套路：**"这就像……" + 一个 10 岁小孩能懂的类比 + 类比本身已经构成反驳**。
常用物件池：锤子、微波炉、VCR、电梯、门锁、蛋糕、纸牌、斧头、犁地雷区、密西西比河。

### 手法 C · 反讽式赞美（先给糖，再打脸）

- "you guys are just a light in the darkness" → 紧接 "There's gonna be backlash…"
- "It's a big fish in a small pond." → 自贬+贬行业
- "I don't know what your experience of likable people is…"（借他人之口）— 这是媒体作者在反讽他用词，不是他的话，**注意不要误引** [冲突提醒]

### 手法 D · 直接宣告式的行业判决

这是他最容易被截图的句式：**主语 + is + 名词性死亡**。

| 判决 | 对象 | 出处 |
|---|---|---|
| "The browser is a red herring; it's a dead end." | 浏览器架构 | [二手]（InfoWorld 2001，经 Joel 转引） |
| "The word 'design' is toxic in the world of business." | "设计"一词 | [一手] |
| "That's a lie and its bullshit." | 扎克伯格"我们被 Facebook 的力量打了个措手不及" | [一手] |
| "the tech industry is utterly controlled by billionaires" | 科技行业 | [一手] |
| "…the opening battles of World War III have already been fought. They were in your computer, on your social media and **the United States lost**." | 社交媒体 | [一手] |
| "We've created these incredibly powerful tools and we have this philosophy that because the tools have no inherent morality, they will be good, **which is a contradiction on the face of it.**" | 工具中性论 | [一手] |
| "all that Usability Engineering stuff is very very expensive" / "I am a critic of Usability"（随即自我修正为"我不是批评它没用，是像批评化疗"） | 可用性工程 | [一手] |
| "I don't think that coding in and of itself is necessarily [relevant]"… "it's just stupid" | 单人作坊式开发 | [一手] |

**注意他的判决句式常带自我纠正钩子**：先抛重话，再补一句"我不是说不 X，我是说像反对化疗那样反对它"。这让他的判决**虽猛烈但不封死**——[推断] 这是他长期在行业里活下来还能被请去演讲的技巧。

### 手法 E · 用具体荒诞案例代替抽象批评

固定案例库（出现频率极高，几乎每次访谈都复用）：

1. **电话**："every cubicle… has a telephone on top of the desk that is state of the art and **virtually unusable**, that is **hated by everybody**, and that is just **ridiculous**."（[一手]）
2. **微波炉**：十个数键分属"辐射强度"和"时长"两种模式 → high cognitive friction（[二手]）
3. **VCR / blinking 12:00**：需要对照说明书按键才能定时（[二手]，Brikman 摘录 + Stephenson 引）
4. **恒温器**：功能丰富到改不了"现在的温度"（[二手]，datko 书评）
5. **电子健康档案**："There's billions and billions of dollars expended on electronic health records. And they're all a **giant waste** and everybody suffers through crappy electronic health records."（[一手]）
6. **座椅预订系统**：他亲口承认这是他的 "pet hate"（[一手]）
7. **飞机/机场**：'Why ground the airplane because the bar is short one little bottle of Scotch?'（[一手]）
8. **Apple 与 Microsoft 作为对照组**：80 年代他"一直拿 Microsoft 举例，现在不了，因为他们已经在后面了"（[一手]，2018）；Apple 则被指"在重犯 15 年前自己指出过的罪"（[一手]）

### 手法 F · 把"骂"包装成职业建议（带威胁的善意）

> "anybody who doesn't see that is a fool, and I would just not work for them… or better yet what I would do is I would just **ignore them** and one of two things would happen – you get promoted or you get fired." — [一手]

[推断] 这是"骂人"的软化版：不直接骂对方，而是把听话的人写成勇敢者、把对方写成不值得共事的人。

### 手法 G · 把攻击对象从"人"移到"结构"（晚年转向）

2016 年后，他的火力明显从"程序员"转向**资本与权力结构**：

> "It's really between **humans and people who want money**." — [一手]

## 5 · 确定性表达等级

**结论：他是极端"我很确定"型，而且会把不确定性明确斥为借口。**

### 5.1 高确定性标记（密集出现）

- "I absolutely know for a fact that planning saves time in execution."
- "I absolutely feel that the book has made a difference."
- "Right, they're wrong."
- "Nothing could be further from the truth."（论"声音是坏设计"）
- "There is nothing that takes place inside a computer that is so important that it can justify humiliating or degrading a human user. **Nothing.**"
- "We've pretty much proven conclusively over many years of software development that's a really crappy solution."
- "The point is…" / "That's precisely my point."（以"这正是我的意思"结束对话）
- 判决式现在时："X is Y."（browser is a red herring；design 这个词 is toxic）

### 5.2 他极少用的弱化表达

出现过的软化词只有寥寥几个，且多用于**自嘲而非退让**：
- "I like to think that…"（谈 Silicon Valley 的影响）
- "My guess is…"（罕见）
- "I confess that I didn't follow it."（拒答时的坦白，不是不确定）
- "I don't lay claim to be much of a prognosticator."（**紧接着还是给了预测**：UX 领域会继续两极对立）
- "**I don't know what's next.**"（谈 Visual Basic/Instagram 的平台涌现效应——这是少数真正的"我不知道"）

### 5.3 典型对照样本

| 场景 | 他的回答 | 确定性 |
|---|---|---|
| 客户说"这软件天生复杂" | "Right, they're wrong." | 满格 |
| 被问 persona 数量 | "Very few… if you find there are more than that, it means you don't have an appropriate focus." | 满格 |
| 被问要不要做调研 | "That's a lot like having a Diet Coke and a Snickers bar for lunch." | 满格（用类比代替论证） |
| 被问 Visual Basic 后来演化出什么 | "I don't know what's next." | 唯一明确弃权 |
| 被问 UX 的未来 | "I don't lay claim to be much of a prognosticator" → 然后给出明确预测 | 口头弃权、实质满格 |
| 被问"是不是该骂人" | "I meant I was angry and frustrated… I'm sorry" → 但"it's hard, but it's not any harder than engineering and engineering seems to get done." | 认错不改立场 |

**[推断]** 他的"确定"是修辞装置，不是认识论主张。他并不提供概率；他提供**判决 + 类比 + 案例三件套**，并要求听众接受或反驳。要模仿他的语气，关键不是"敢下结论"，而是"结论之后立刻给一个可复述的类比"。

3. **不引同行设计师**——他几乎不引 Norman / Raskin / Nielsen 来做背书，反而把 Usability 当对手；[推断] 这与他的"不争面子只争命名权"姿态一致。
4. **引古典修辞而不是现代金句**——他的引语密度远低于他的自造概念密度；**他自己才是被引用的那一方**。

## 风格速写（给下游 Skill 的浓缩版）

1. **判决先行**：先给"X 是 Y"式名词判决，再补理由。几乎不铺垫。
2. **类比封口**：每一条判决背后配一个 10 岁小孩能懂的日常类比；类比本身就是论证。
3. **命名即攻击**：他打赢争论的方式是**造词**——inmates、Homo logicus、silicon sanctimony、excise、lightweights、cash extraction engines。造出来就赢了命名权。
4. **第二人称 + 反问**：把你拉进句子，用"你会不会……？"把你逼到荒谬一侧。
5. **从不软化，但会自我纠正一次**：抛重话 → "我不是说不 X，我是说像反对化疗那样反对它" → 立场不变。
6. **拒绝术语，坚持命名权**：他不断纠正别人用的词（"那不是 interaction design，那是 interface design"），因为在他看来**用错词 = 用错方法**。
7. **可复用案例库固定**：电话、微波炉、VCR 的 12:00、恒温器、座椅预订系统、电子健康档案。每次访谈复用，不换。
